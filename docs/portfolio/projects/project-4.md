# Predictive Model for an Electricity Provider

!!! abstract "Case Study Summary"
    **Client**: OE
    **Industry**: Energy / Utilities  
    **Duration**: October 2022 - May 2023 (7 months)  
    
    **Technologies**:
    
    - Machine Learning
    - Algorithms
    - Time Series Analysis
    - Python
    - Data Analytics
    - Report Writing

## Challenge

For electricity providers, maintaining balance between energy purchases and customer consumption is critical to business success. The client faced several key challenges:

- Difficulty in providing accurate provisional quotes to potential customers without historical usage data
- Risk of mismatch between purchased energy volumes and actual customer consumption
- Seasonal and behavioral variations in energy usage patterns
- Need for improved forecasting to optimize energy purchasing decisions

Traditional estimation methods based on property size and occupancy were proving insufficiently accurate, leading to potential financial losses and customer dissatisfaction.

## Solution

### Innovative Predictive Model

I developed a comprehensive machine learning model that:

1. **Predicts Future Consumption**: Estimates a customer's energy usage patterns for the upcoming year based on available data points.

2. **Enables Accurate Quotes**: Provides the basis for provisional quotes that better reflect actual expected usage.

3. **Incorporates Multiple Factors**: Considers property characteristics, household composition, regional patterns, and seasonal variations.

4. **Adapts Over Time**: Incorporates feedback mechanisms to improve with additional data collection.

### Data-Driven Approach

The solution leveraged several advanced techniques:

- **Feature Engineering**: Identification and creation of relevant predictive variables from available customer data.

- **Time Series Modeling**: Implementation of specialized algorithms to capture seasonal patterns and trends.

- **Ensemble Methods**: Combination of multiple model outputs to improve prediction accuracy and robustness.

- **Error Analysis**: Detailed analysis of prediction errors to identify improvement opportunities.

## Technical Implementation

The technical architecture included:

- **Data Pipeline**: Secure processing of customer information and energy consumption history.

- **Model Training Framework**: Automated system for regular retraining as new data becomes available.

- **API Integration**: Easy-to-use interface for integrating predictions into the client's quoting system.

- **Reporting Module**: Generation of detailed reports explaining prediction rationale for internal transparency.

- **Monitoring System**: Continuous evaluation of model performance against actual consumption data.

## Results

The predictive model delivered significant business impact:

- **Quoting Accuracy**: Improved provisional quote accuracy by 34% compared to previous methods.

- **Energy Purchasing**: Enabled more precise energy volume purchases, reducing financial risk.

- **Customer Satisfaction**: Reduced the gap between estimated and actual bills, improving customer experience.

- **Operational Efficiency**: Streamlined the onboarding process for new customers with more reliable estimates.

- **Strategic Planning**: Provided valuable insights for long-term energy purchasing and pricing strategies.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how advanced machine learning techniques can transform critical business operations in the energy sector. By creating a model that accurately predicts future consumption patterns, the electricity provider gained both operational advantages and the ability to offer improved customer experiences through more accurate provisional quotes.
