# Featured Projects

Welcome to my portfolio of AI engineering projects. Each case study highlights my approach to solving real business challenges through innovative AI solutions.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   [RAG Chatbot for a Digital Company](projects/project-1.md)

    ---

    Development of an intelligent chatbot based on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology for a digital personal development company. The solution dynamically retrieves information from a knowledge base and generates coherent, precise responses tailored to user needs.

-   [Pricing Model Optimization for E-Commerce](projects/project-2.md)

    ---

    Created an automated pricing model for an e-commerce company to optimize gross margin across their extensive product catalog. The solution considers seasonality and historical sales data to determine optimal pricing, replacing a manual, time-consuming process.

-   [AI Agents for Legal Documentation](projects/project-3.md)

    ---

    Designed AI agents capable of automatically identifying relevant legal sources and integrating them into generated legal documents. Built with a Flask API and ElasticSearch engine using hybrid search strategies combining keywords and vector embeddings.

-   [Predictive Model for an Electricity Provider](projects/project-4.md)

    ---

    Developed an innovative Machine Learning predictive model to estimate future energy consumption of customers for an electricity provider, enabling the company to provide provisional quotes based on projected annual usage.
